# Aurora Marketplace

Aurora Marketplace is a digital marketplace for motorcycle spare parts, designed to connect suppliers, retailers, mechanics, riders, and other participants in the motorcycle aftermarket ecosystem.

The platform aims to make finding, buying, selling, and managing motorcycle spare parts easier, safer, and more efficient.

## Overview

Aurora Marketplace provides a centralized platform where users can:

- Search for motorcycle spare parts
- Compare products and prices
- Purchase products securely
- Track orders and deliveries
- Manage inventory
- Create digital supplier stores
- Manage sales and customers
- Access business insights

The long-term goal is to build a trusted digital ecosystem for the motorcycle spare parts industry in Tanzania and eventually across East Africa.

## Main Users

Aurora is designed for:

- Motorcycle spare parts suppliers
- Importers and wholesalers
- Retail spare parts shops
- Mechanics
- Bodaboda riders
- Motorcycle owners
- Fleet operators
- Logistics providers
